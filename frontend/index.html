Privacy & Security Notice

  • Local Processing: All detection and embedding generation occurs strictly on this machine. No data is transmitted to external cloud APIs.
  • Data Minimization: Raw images are discarded immediately after feature extraction. Only 112x112 thumbnails and mathematical vectors are retained.
  • Consent: Operators are responsible for securing consent prior to biometric enrollment.
  • Right to Erasure: Deleting an identity from the People directory immediately purges all associated vector and thumbnail data from the SQLite index.
